## 一、概述

语篇是在一定语境下，形式上具有衔接性、语义上具有连贯性的口头或书面语言。

语篇教学的基本内容为语篇结构特征、语言特征、文化内涵和交际意义。具体内容为结合词汇、词法、句法等知识理解语篇的含义，概括文章主旨大意、段落大意，分析文中关键词句、修辞手法及其意义、语法结构及其作用，梳理关键词句的逻辑关系并推断其隐含意义。

语篇是表达意义的语言单位，语篇知识是发展语言运用能力的基础。在语篇教学过程中需充分利用多模态形式的语篇，如文字、图示、歌曲等，指导学生欣赏与教材同等难度的小说节选、剧本节选、散文、经典演讲、名人传记、电影等，使学生增加语言积累，体验语言的文化内涵，逐步领悟语言的多元功能，提高逻辑思维能力。

## 三、教学指引

因为创作目的的不同，每个语篇的文体也不尽相同。不同的文体在基本结构和表达方法上各有特点。教学中可以设计预测、略读、扫读、详读、细听、笔记、概述等方法，引导学生关注文体特征、抓住主旨大意、判断作者立场、获取基本信息、理解关键词句并推断其隐含意义。除了教材中阅读、视听说、写作等板块以外，教师还可以指导学生广泛阅读各类报纸杂志，聆听故事、演讲等形式多样的语篇表达方式，在大量阅读和视听的基础上开展仿写、缩写、续写、创作等活动，在实践中体会不同文体在谋篇布局中的异同，以及在交际中的不同功能和作用。

### （二）要点解析

#### 5.1 记叙文

记叙文是以记人、叙事为主，把人物的经历和事情的发展变化过程表达出来的一种文体形式。有效的记叙通常有一个鲜明的主题，通过叙事传达作者的观点。

##### 5.1.1 基本结构

写记叙文就是讲故事，无论是虚构的还是真实的故事都会围绕“发生了什么”这个问题展开。每个故事通常都有情节、人物、场景、叙事人称等基本要素构成。记叙文围绕着一个或一系列事件展开，按一定的叙述顺序交代事件的起因、发展、高潮和结果。叙事的人称可以是第一人称（真实可信）或第三人称（客观灵活）。

##### 5.1.2 主旨大意、段落大意

1. 叙事目的

通常作者在叙述之前都有一个明确的叙事目的，即文章的主旨大意。把握好记叙文的主旨对理解文章起到至关重要的作用。例如，在读到一个关于老师在课堂上公开向学生们承认讲错知识内容的故事时，读者就需要明确作者的写作目的是为了证明老师的教学水平太低还是为了说明老师具有勇于承担、知错就改的品质，这样就能比较正确地把握文章的主旨，体会作者的情感。

2. 叙述顺序

大多数记叙文会按照事件发生、发展的时间先后顺序来进行叙述。用这种顺叙方法进行叙述的好处是事件由头到尾，次序井然，文章自然贯通，条理清楚。

但有时也会根据表达的需要，把事件的结局或某个最重要、最突出的片段提到文章的前面，然后再从事件的开头按事情先后发展顺序进行叙述，这种倒叙手法广泛运用于电影和小说的创作中。用倒叙能增强文章的生动性，使文章产生悬念，更能引人入胜。当倒叙的部分叙述完毕回到顺叙时一定会有过渡句或过渡段把时间交代清楚，否则会使文章头绪不清，影响理解。

##### 5.1.3 &#42;关键词句、隐含意义

在阅读记叙文时，除了要准确把握关键词语的意义及用法外，还需要理解个别句子的深层含义。在很多记叙文中，句子不仅有其表面意思，还有暗含的深层次意思，即句子在一定语境中所隐含的意义，也就是人们常说的言外之意、弦外之音。探求句子的深层含义通常需要了解作者的写作意图和背景，联系文章的写作特点，结合人物的性格特点来分析推断。

##### The Natural Garden

① There once lived a king who had a palace with beautiful gardens. In those gardens lived thousands of creatures representing hundreds of different species. It was a paradise.

② There was only one thing that the king disliked — the remains of a huge tree standing in the middle of the gardens. It was withered and dry. So, he had it cut down and replaced with elaborate fountains.

③ Some time later, a wise nobleman visited the king. “Your Majesty, people have been singing the praises of the beauty of these gardens and the many creatures that live in them. But I've hardly seen a thing move — not one animal. I feel tricked!” whispered the nobleman. The king realised that the wise man was right! The animals had left and the gardens were empty. The king sent for his advisers. He offered a reward to anyone with a solution. He was presented with many theories and ideas. He tried them all, but nothing could restore the royal gardens to their former splendour.

④ Years later, long after many of the grounds' plants and flowers had also disappeared or withered, a young man showed up. He had an explanation. “There just wasn't enough poo， Your Majesty. Particularly, moth poo.”

⑤ Everyone broke into laughter. The man was a fool. The guards were ready to throw him out, but the king stopped them. “I want to hear what you have to say.”

⑥ The man carefully explained how everything in a garden is connected. The bigger animals fed mainly on the brightly coloured birds, which ate colourful worms, which in turn fed on the various plants and flowers native to that part of the world. These rare plants and flowers flourished amongst insects and other organisms as long as there was enough moth poo. But how could a young man know so much about the royal gardens?

⑦ It turned out that before he was born, his father had collected the old tree that the king ordered taken out. He replanted it on their land. Every spring, thousands of moths came out of the tree and the beauty of the royal gardens recreated itself there.

⑧ Now that the king knew what was wrong and how it could be fixed, he wanted his gardens back. But recreating the gardens, he later found out, would take many years. Nature couldn't be rushed. It would take time for balance to be restored and the king would not live to see it. The king was full of regrets. He had so carelessly destroyed the delicate balance of nature.

语篇讲述了国王和他的花园的故事。国王原本有一座美丽的花园，但他觉得一棵丑陋的枯树有煞风景，因而下令将其移走。没过多久，花园中的各种小动物和鸟类都离开了。花园的生态失去了平衡，也失去了生机与美丽。国王知道了原因后追悔莫及。语篇的主旨在于引导读者思考自然界中万物之间的平衡关系，并警示生态平衡一旦遭到破坏，人类将付出巨大的代价。

故事采用了记叙文中较为常用的按照事件发生、发展的时间先后顺序进行叙述的方法。 第一和第二段交代了故事背景。作者先使用了寓言故事较为常用的“There once lived a king …”，表明故事发生在久远的过去。接着，交代了故事的起因：国王下令砍了花园中那棵丑陋的树。第三至第七段是故事的发展和高潮部分：国王发现花园毫无生机，却束手无策，直到一个年轻人道出其中原因。在第三段和第四段的开头，分别使用了时间状语“Some time later”和“Years later, long after…”，使故事推进的线索更清晰明朗。最后一段以国王追悔莫及结束全文，同时引发读者思考。故事通篇以第三人称叙事。

文章最后一段中有这样两句话：“Nature couldn't be rushed. It would take time for balance to be restored and the king would not live to see it.”字面意思是自然界的东西是急不来的，修复被破坏的平衡是需要时间的，国王有生之年可能看不到了。但是，它还有深层次的含义需要读者仔细品味，包括我们需要尊重大自然万物的生长规律，了解大自然万物的关系以及善待大自然。

#### 5.2 说明文

##### 5.2.1 说明方法

说明文是客观地解释说明具体事物、阐明抽象概念、复杂过程或事理的一种文体。它与
人们的生产、工作和生活的关系相当密切，是运用范围极为广泛的常用文体。通常它的内容
具有高度科学性，结构具有清晰的条理性，语言具有严密准确性。

为了把事物特征说清楚，或者把概念事理阐述明白，必须有相适应的说明方法。常见的说明方法有下定义、举例子、分类别、作比较等。

1. 下定义

下定义是一种用简洁明确的语言对事物的本质特征作概括的说明方法。

例如：高中《英语》（上教版）必修第一册 Unit 1 Our world 中 How to study culture 一文中写道：“A cultural idea refers to an understanding of the world that typically reflects the way of thinking in a particular culture.”这里对“文化观念”作出了通俗易懂的定义，即它是对世界的理解（an understanding of the world），尤其能反映出某个特定文化或社会（a particular culture）的思维方式（the way of thinking）。

2. 举例子

举例子是通过列举有代表性的、恰当的事例来说明事物特征的说明方法。当要解释说明一个比较复杂的概念或抽象的事理时，需要举一些具体的例子来帮助读者理解。

以高中《英语》（上教版）必修第二册 Unit 3 Progress?中 The young entrepreneur's guide 一文为例：

##### The young entrepreneur's guide

Lots of people dream of having their own business, but what do they have to do to become entrepreneurs?

**Have a cool business idea**

You'll enjoy your business more if it does something that really interests you, so use your hobbies for ideas. Sixteen-year-old Michelle Coakley form Ireland loved jewellery and her friend Jade Kearney was interested in environmental issues. Sharing a creative spirit, Michelle and Jade decided to start their own fashion company. Together they designed and sold eco-friendly jewellery, but they faced many difficult challenges and had trouble finding customers at first. Not wanting to give up, the two girls worked incredibly hard and managed to sell €1,500 of jewellery in a short time!

##### E-learning

Courses that are taught via the Internet, or online courses, have become popular in many countries. There are different kinds of online courses, but most show a video of a teacher and students in a classroom. Students watch the video online and take part in online activities. Some courses are free and open to anyone, giving students anywhere the chance to study at any time. Of course, watching students and teachers on a computer screen is not the same as taking part in a real class. Other kinds of e-learning are being tested around the world. In Singapore, eight schools have been chosen to try new ways of using technology in the classroom. The schools have tablets and touchscreens instead of books and blackboards in the classrooms. There are also social media platforms to help the students work together on projects, and online “worlds” where the students can interact with each other and the digital environment while completing different learning tasks. In South Korea, some students have been taught English by teachers in the Philippines and other countries since 2011. The teachers are in their home countries and are connected to the Korean classrooms via the Internet. Instead of looking at a screen, the students interact with a robot in the classroom. The robot is controlled by the teacher and can move around the classroom and talk with the students. It is unlikely that robot teachers will replace real teachers, but like online learning environments, they can be useful tools.

These important examples show us that e-learning can be successful. Thanks to technology, more and more ways of teaching and learning are being developed. Nobody knows what the classroom of the future will look like, but one thing is for sure — there has never been a more interesting time to be a student.

这是一篇介绍线上学习的说明文。作者将线上学习主要分为三大类。第一类是学生可以随时随地通过网络观课并参加教学活动。第二类是学生和教师在课堂中用平板或触摸屏代替书本与黑板进行教与学，学生们可以通过社交平台进行互动，共同完成学习任务。第三类是远在千里之外的老师通过网络给学生授课，学生在课上和由老师操控的机器人进行互动交流。通过分类别的方法，作者根据教学地点，教学工具，互动形式等方面的差异，将复杂多样的线上学习介绍得有条有理，清晰易懂。

作比较是将两个不同的对象加以比较，来说明事物特征的说明方法。

#### 5.3 &#42;议论文

议论文是一种论述事理、发表意见、提出主张的文体。议论是作者对客观事物进行分
析、评论，以表明自己的见解、主张、态度和说服他人的表达方式。议论文通常由论点、论据、
论证三部分构成。明确的论点、充足的论据、合理的论证会使议论文既严密又富有逻辑性。
##### 5.3.1 论点与论据

1. 论点

论点是正确鲜明地阐述作者观点的句子，是议论文的灵魂和统帅。它是整个论证过程的中心，担负着“证明什么”的任务，明确地表示作者赞成什么，反对什么。论点应该正确、鲜明、概括，是一个完整的判断句，绝不可模棱两可。

2. 论据

论据是支撑论点的材料，是作者用来证明论点的理由和根据，承担着“用什么来证明”的任务。论据是为了证明论点，因此，两者的联系应该紧密一致。代表性的事例、确凿的数据、可靠的史实、为社会普遍承认的真理等都可以作为论据。作为论据的材料必须可靠、典型、充分。

注：论证是用严密充足的论据来证明论点的过程，承担着“怎么来证明”的任务。论证的目的在于揭示出论点和论据之间的内在逻辑关系。议论文的论证一般分为立论和驳论两大类型。立论是对一定的事件或问题从正面阐述作者的见解和主张的论证方法。驳论是以有力的论据反驳别人错误论点的论证方式。立论和驳论都是一种证明，无非一个是从正面证明其正确，而另一个是从反面证明其错误。在议论文中这两种论证方法经常被一起使用，在“破”的基础之上同时建立自己的观点。

#### 5.4 应用文

应用文是人类在长期的社会实践活动中形成的一种文体，是人们传递信息、处理事务、
交流感情的工具。它是人们在生活、学习、工作中为处理实际事务而写的具有实用性特点，
并形成惯用格式的文章。
##### 5.4.1 文本格式

不同种类的应用文有其相对固定的格式。例如：日常信件通常由信头（包括地址和日
期）、称呼、正文、信末客套语和签名五个部分组成。以高中《英语》（上教版）必修第一册
